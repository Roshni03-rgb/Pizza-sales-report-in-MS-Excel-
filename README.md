#  🍕 Pizza-sales-report-in-MS-Excel-
Pizza Sales Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel using PivotTables, Pivot Charts, KPIs, slicers, and interactive visuals. Analyzes sales trends, revenue, peak hours, top-selling pizzas, and customer ordering patterns to deliver actionable business insights. This project demonstrates strong data analysis and dashboard design skills.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an **interactive Pizza Sales Dashboard** built entirely in **Microsoft Excel** using **PivotTables, PivotCharts, KPIs, Slicers, Conditional Formatting, and a Sales Heat Map**. The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights, helping stakeholders monitor sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, and product trends.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard answers key business questions such as:

* 💰 What is the total revenue generated?
* 📦 How many orders and pizzas were sold?
* ⏰ Which hours generate the highest sales?
* 📅 How do weekday and weekend sales compare?
* 📈 How does revenue change throughout the year?
* 🍕 Which pizza types are the best and worst sellers?
* 📏 Which pizza sizes are most popular?
* 🔥 Which day and hour combinations generate the highest revenue?

## 🎛 Interactive Features

The dashboard includes dynamic slicers for:

* 📅 Day of Week
* 📆 Month
* 📏 Pizza Size

These filters allow users to explore sales performance from different perspectives in real time.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Peak sales occur during **lunch hours (12 PM–1 PM)**.
* **Weekday sales account for approximately 73%** of total sales.
* **Large pizzas** contribute the highest share of total sales.
* Revenue remains relatively stable throughout the year with noticeable monthly fluctuations.
* A small number of pizza varieties generate most of the sales, while several contribute comparatively less.
